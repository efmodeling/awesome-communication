# awesome-communication

A curated list of science communication resources — papers, guides, and books to help scientists communicate their work effectively.

---

## Writing & Presenting Science

- **[How to Write/Present Science: BABY-WEREWOLF-SILVER BULLET](https://ecoevoevoeco.blogspot.com/2014/10/how-to-writepresent-science-baby.html)**  
  A blog post by Andrew Hendry explaining a memorable framework (Baby → Werewolf → Silver Bullet → Dead Werewolf) for structuring papers, proposals, and presentations to be compelling and clear.

- **[Writing Tips for Scientists (PDF)](https://redpath-staff.mcgill.ca/hendry/writingtips.pdf)**  
  A comprehensive PDF guide from McGill University (Andrew Hendry) with practical, actionable tips on all aspects of scientific writing.

- **[Grant Writing and Written Communication — Northwestern CLIMB](https://www.northwestern.edu/climb/resources/written-communication/)**  
  Science-based resources on written communication including grant proposals (NIH/NSF), abstracts, figures, and tables. Includes workshop slides and video files.

- **[Hybrid Pointing Rehearsal Record](https://light-tracer.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/hybrid-pointing-rehearsal-record-open-v1.pdf)**  
  A free two-page CC BY 4.0 worksheet for checking indicated slide details across room, shared-screen and recorded views before a hybrid presentation. Published by Light Tracer; AI-assisted.
